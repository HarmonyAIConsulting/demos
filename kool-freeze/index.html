Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Common causes include a dirty filter, a frozen coil, low refrigerant or a failed outdoor fan or part. Turn the system off if you see ice, and call for service.

How often should my HVAC system be serviced?

Most systems benefit from a check once a year, and heat pumps that run all year often do best with a spring and a fall visit. Coastal salt air can make regular service even more important.

How often should I change my air filter?

It depends on the filter and your home. Many 1-inch filters need changing every 1 to 3 months, more often with pets or during heavy use.

Should I repair or replace my system?

Age, repair cost, efficiency and how often it breaks down all matter. A technician can walk you through both options honestly.
